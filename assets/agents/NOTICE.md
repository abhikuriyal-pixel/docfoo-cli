# Coding-agent logos

These assets come from each project's official website or vendor-published
Visual Studio Marketplace listing, not a third-party icon collection.
Exact source URLs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `SOURCES.json`.

- Pi: official `pi.dev/logo-auto.svg`, also referenced by the Pi repository README.
- OpenCode: official dark square SVG download from `opencode.ai/brand`; its
  encoded data URI was decoded without changing its paths or colors.
- Codex: OpenAI's icon from the official `openai.chatgpt` extension listing.
- Claude Code: Anthropic's icon from the official `anthropic.claude-code` listing.

Icons are displayed upright and unmodified. The diamond outlines are separate
layout containers, not changes to the logos. Product names and marks belong to
their respective owners; use here identifies shell/skill integration, not
sponsorship or endorsement. The site's MIT license does not relicense these
trademarks.
