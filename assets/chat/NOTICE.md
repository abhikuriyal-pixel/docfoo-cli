# Messaging artwork and phone compositions

`telegram-logo.svg` and `telegram-pattern.svg` are unchanged copies of the
artwork used by the PiKins landing page, originally obtained from Telegram:

- Logo: https://telegram.org/img/t_logo.svg
  SHA-256: `85059d5e5bf7bda91ebab30664993c49867a26be6b947834aca16c846581766a`
- Wallpaper: https://telegram.org/img/tgme/pattern.svg?1
  SHA-256: `118add53487c02aaf5b5ab9f69380fa06717deb10492e14aaa487e3c62806ad4`

These are Telegram’s artwork and branding, not MIT-licensed site artwork.
They identify the optional Telegram integration. No endorsement is implied.
The Hermes girl and PiKins marks retain the notices in `../brand/NOTICE.md`.
`slack-logo.svg` is the unchanged four-color Slack mark by Slack Technologies,
retrieved from Wikimedia Commons, whose source attribution points to Slack’s
official brand asset collection:

- Source attribution: https://brandfolder.com/slack/logos
- File/provenance: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Slack_icon_2019.svg
- Download: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/d5/Slack_icon_2019.svg
- SHA-256: `17160477a475166ad0556e0028e3b0bd0634d70c02e7b920c04a4429ea184ab3`
- Current official media kit and usage terms: https://slack.com/media-kit

Commons identifies the simple logo as public-domain artwork; Slack trademark
rights still apply. It is used in its original colors solely to identify the
Slack integration. No endorsement is implied. The send/attachment glyphs are
site UI artwork, not additional product logos.

The phone frame and Telegram interface styling are adapted from the PiKins
landing page (MIT, copyright 2026 abhikuriyal-pixel). Both phone conversations
are static HTML/CSS compositions, not screenshots or recordings of live
Telegram or Slack sessions. Their FlashAttention answer excerpt and source
figure reuse this site’s existing recorded response and credited paper asset;
they are not separate captures or benchmarks. Routing messages are authored
UI copy. `tools/prepare-data.py` assembles the public HTML.

The compositions make no network requests and contain no chat credentials,
private messages, phone numbers, account identifiers or workspace details.
